A note for you, Mama

There are some people in this world who make everything feel safe, warm, and full of love — and you are the person who has always been that for me.

You never let me fall without catching me. You celebrated every little win as if it were the world's greatest achievement. You taught me kindness not by telling me about it, but by being the most generous, patient, beautiful soul I have ever known.

On this day — and honestly, every single day — I want you to know that I see you. I see all the quiet sacrifices, the late nights, the countless ways you have poured yourself into us, into me. And I am so endlessly grateful to be your child.

Thank you for your hugs that fix everything. For your voice that sounds like home. For your laugh that fills the whole room. For being you.

I love you more than any words I could ever write — but I will keep trying anyway. Happy Mother's Day, Mama. 🌸

— Always yours ❤️
